Dishwasher with an Improved Spray Arm Assembly

Publication number: US 2024/0123456 A1  ·  Filed: 2023-06-01  ·  Assignee: Example Appliance Co.

Summary

A dishwasher comprises a washing chamber, a sump arranged at the bottom of the chamber, and a spray arm rotatably supported above the sump. A control unit is configured to operate a circulation pump so that washing liquid is supplied to the spray arm at a variable pressure.

The spray arm includes a plurality of nozzles arranged along its upper surface. At least one nozzle is inclined relative to the vertical axis, so that the washing liquid is directed toward a corner region of the chamber that conventional arrangements fail to reach.

Detailed Description

According to one embodiment, the control unit determines a load condition of the dishes placed in the chamber and adjusts the rotation speed of the circulation pump accordingly. When a light load is detected, the pump operates at a reduced speed, thereby lowering both noise and energy consumption.

The control unit may further receive a signal from a turbidity sensor arranged in the sump. If the turbidity exceeds a predetermined threshold, the duration of the main washing cycle is extended by a fixed interval. This arrangement avoids extending the cycle unnecessarily when the washing liquid remains substantially clean.
